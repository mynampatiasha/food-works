About ABRA FOOD

One Brand. Every Taste. Every Table. Everywhere.

ABRA FOOD is a global hospitality platform built around three dining experiences — ABRA VEG, ABRA NON-VEG and ABRA CAFÉ — with hotels, catering and a food store on the way.

Our Story

From One Kitchen to a World of Taste

ABRA FOOD began with a simple idea: great food shouldn't be split across a dozen different brands and apps. Whether a guest wants a wholesome vegetarian thali, a smoky tandoori feast, or a quiet coffee and croissant — it should all live under one trusted name.

What started as a single restaurant has grown into a multi-brand hospitality platform spanning cities and countries, built on one belief: consistency, quality and warmth at every table, everywhere ABRA goes.

Food Philosophy

Cooked Fresh. Served with Care.

Every ABRA kitchen follows the same standard — fresh ingredients, standardised recipes, and chefs trained across our three brands. Whether it's a South Indian breakfast or a Gulf-style grill, the promise is the same: it tastes like ABRA, everywhere.

Quality & Safety

Held to One Standard, Globally

All ABRA outlets follow strict food safety protocols, regular hygiene audits and standardised sourcing — so every dish, in every country, meets the same bar.

Sustainability

Growing Responsibly

From reducing food waste in our kitchens to sourcing locally wherever possible, ABRA is building a platform that scales without losing sight of its footprint.

Local Sourcing

Partnering with local farms and suppliers in every market we enter.

Waste Reduction

Daily inventory discipline to minimise food waste across outlets.

Sustainable Packaging

Moving toward biodegradable packaging across delivery & takeaway.
